1 Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how K3K3ride may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit https://www.k3k3ride.com/ or use our online services. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies? Cookies are small files stored in your browser or device. Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, pixels or other tools used to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve security or understand service use. The technologies actually used depend on features and third-party services enabled on the platform.

2 Why We May Use Cookies

Essential functionality and security technologies may support session management, account authentication, security and protection against misuse. Where strictly necessary for a service requested by the user, they may operate without separate opt-in where permitted by law.

Preference technologies may remember language, regional preferences or other settings where available. If analytics tools are enabled, they may help us understand visits, usage patterns, errors and performance. If marketing technologies are introduced, they may help measure campaigns or support promotions. Non-essential analytics or marketing technologies should be used only where implemented and where required consent or other legal conditions are satisfied.

3 Third-Party Technologies

Some functions may rely on hosting, mapping, payment, analytics or customer-support services. Depending on how they are integrated, those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies governed by their own policies. K3K3ride will seek to assess relevant integrations and provide information where required.

4 Managing Your Choices

You may manage cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Where K3K3ride provides a cookie preference tool, you may use it to manage non-essential technologies. If consent is required, non-essential technologies should not be activated before consent is obtained. Blocking essential technologies may affect account access or other functions.

Note on Local Storage: Changing browser settings may not remove all data stored through other technologies, such as local storage; these may need to be cleared separately through your browser's developer or privacy clearing tools.

5 Location Services

Ride requests and location-based features may require access to your device's location. Location permissions are controlled through your device or browser settings. Location data used to provide rides is addressed in our Privacy Policy. Not all location processing relies on cookies, and disabling cookies does not necessarily disable device location services. If you deny location access, you may need to enter pickup or destination details manually where available.

6 Cookie Duration

Session cookies generally expire when the browser session ends. Persistent cookies remain for a defined period or until deleted. Duration depends on the specific technology and provider. K3K3ride does not assign one retention period to all cookies; where applicable, details should be provided through the cookie preference tool or other appropriate disclosures.

7 Personal Data and Cookies

Some cookies or similar technologies may collect information linked to an individual, such as online identifiers or device information. Where this constitutes personal data, it will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable Ghanaian data protection law. We aim to limit collection to what is necessary and provide appropriate choices where required.

8 Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this policy when our website, technologies or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be published with an updated date. Where required, we will update cookie preferences or provide additional notice.
