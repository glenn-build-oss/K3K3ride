Help & Insights

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, helpful answers about rides, fares, driver onboarding, and safety with K3K3.

How does K3K3 work for passengers?

Simply sign in with your phone number, choose your pickup station across Ho or university campuses (HTU, UHAS), and confirm your request. You'll be matched with a verified nearby tricycle driver within minutes.

What payment methods are supported?

We support transfers across MTN , Telecel Cash and cash payments. Fares are straightforward with zero hidden fees, surge pricing, or payment deductions.

Are K3K3 drivers and vehicles verified?

Yes. All K3K3 drivers undergo identity verification, licensing checks, and vehicle safety inspections before their accounts are authorized to accept trips.

How do I apply to become a K3K3 driver?

Open the Driver Portal from the navigation or quick links, create an account, and fill out your vehicle and licensing details. Our administration team will review and approve your account.

What happens after submitting my driver application?

Your application moves to our administration queue under pending review. You will receive an SMS/notification on your phone once your account has been approved or if any further details are required.

Which areas and stations are covered?

K3K3 currently operates across Ho township, connecting university campuses (HTU, UHAS), student hostels, medical centers, and main market corridors on demand.
